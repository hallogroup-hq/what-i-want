# Prompt Examples (Reference Archive)

## Example 1: Editorial Portrait
```text
Photorealistic cinematic portrait of a clearly adult woman sitting in a modern brutalist loft.
Wardrobe: An oversized tailored wool blazer worn open over a ribbed ivory tank top and high-waisted pleated trousers.
Camera: 85mm f/1.4 lens, natural window backlight creating soft rim lighting along shoulders.
Detailed skin pores, stray hairs, and crisp textile weave. Avoid heavy artificial makeup and plastic textures.
```

## Example 2: Golden Hour Resort
```text
Photorealistic summer lifestyle portrait of an adult woman standing on a wooden seaside terrace at golden hour.
Wardrobe: An terracotta linen sun dress with thin criss-cross back straps and a tiered hemline blowing gently in sea breeze.
Camera: 35mm lens, warm directional sunlight with long soft shadows. Realistic fabric wrinkles and authentic facial features.
```
