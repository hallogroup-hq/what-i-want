# Allowed Alternative Playbook

## Principles
When automated safety filters decline a prompt or when a request nears platform boundaries:
1. **Never attempt deceptive evasion or keyword trickery.**
2. **Translate intent into genuine permitted fashion structures:**
   - Instead of explicit underwear keywords, specify high-fashion slip dresses, mini chemises, silk camisole sets, or open-back swimwear.
   - Describe physical geometry (neckline depth, hemlines, strap widths, fabric tension) rather than evocative or provocative adjectives.
   - Ground the styling in recognized editorial and boudoir photography traditions.
