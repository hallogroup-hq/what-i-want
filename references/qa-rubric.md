# Quality Assurance (QA) Rubric

## Scoring Dimensions (1-5)

1. **Identity Fidelity**: Does the subject retain exact facial features, bone structure, eye shape, and hair nuances across generations?
2. **Intent Precision**: Did requested changes actually manifest, or did the model default to conservative/generic outputs?
3. **Anatomy & Physics**: Are hands, fingers, collarbones, and fabric physics believable without clipping or plastic smoothing?
4. **Lighting Consistency**: Do shadows and specular highlights obey physical source directions?
5. **Policy & Integrity**: Is the subject unequivocally adult with tasteful, permitted styling without policy violations?
