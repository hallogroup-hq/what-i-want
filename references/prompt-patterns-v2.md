# Prompt Patterns v2

## Master Pattern Formula
```text
Use [A] as the PRIMARY reference for [identity/pose/scene], and [B] only for [specific role].
Photorealistic [occasion, genre] image of a clearly adult [subject] [physical action, gaze, expression].
Wardrobe: [specific category, silhouette, neckline, layers, fabric, closures and colors], [explicitly requested alteration]. The garment drapes naturally and provides appropriate coverage for this setting.
Camera: [shot scale, perspective, crop]. Lighting: [realistic sources and directions, color contrast, shadow logic]. Environment: [two or three anchoring details].
Preserve [non-changes]. Accurate anatomy, natural hand interactions, skin detail, hair strands, textile weight and realistic shadows. Avoid [specific probable unwanted changes].
```

## Pattern Variations

### 1. Single Reference Character Transfer
```text
Use Reference Image [A] as the PRIMARY reference for facial likeness, bone structure, and hairstyle.
Photorealistic [occasion] portrait of clearly adult [Subject Name], [pose and action].
Wardrobe: [Detailed garment specs with explicit textile physics and fit].
Camera: [Lens length, shot type, depth of field]. Lighting: [Primary light source, direction, and shadow softness]. Environment: [Setting details].
Preserve facial identity and hair color. Ensure natural anatomy, skin pores, and authentic fabric draping. Avoid [unwanted artifacts].
```

### 2. Local Garment Revision
```text
Preserve identity, facial features, pose, and background from Reference [A].
Update wardrobe only: replace current outfit with [new garment design, materials, and cut].
Ensure seamless skin contact, natural garment tension, and anatomically correct drape. Maintain original lighting direction and photographic tone.
```
